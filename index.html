ⓘ About this tracker

Hello and welcome! I created this adoptable tracker for myself because I was getting a little too lazy to keep everything organized in Excel, lol. It was inspired by commission trackers I found online, and I thought, why not make one specifically for adoptables?

Feel free to use this tracker for your own personal records. It includes:

  • Adoptable and buyer name/username records
  • Price, currency, payment status, payment method, selling type, and purchase type
  • Ownership and resale details, notes, and an optional adoptable file or link
  • Multiple proof-of-purchase attachments, including images and PDFs
  • Automatically generated receipts that you can print or save as PDF, plus downloadable receipt files
  • Search, filters, light and dark mode, and editable tracker settings with a profile picture
  • JSON backup and restore, CSV export, and a 30-day Recently Deleted recovery area
  • On hold and partially paid payment plans with target dates

Important storage note: Your records and attached proof files are saved locally in the browser on the device you use. They do not automatically sync between devices. Clearing browser/site data or resetting or uninstalling the browser may erase them, so export a JSON backup regularly.
